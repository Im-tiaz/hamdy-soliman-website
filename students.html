Undergraduate Researchers

Dr. Soliman has mentored numerous undergraduate students through independent research projects, senior design projects, and externally funded research initiatives, particularly through the NSF-funded Smart & Secure Sensor Lab. Undergraduate researchers have contributed to projects involving wireless sensor network deployments, field experiments in forest fire emulation and border intrusion detection, neural network modeling for image compression, and machine learning applications in healthcare.
