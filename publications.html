Quantifying the Stability–Recovery–Interpretability Trade-Off Between K-Means and Self-Organizing Maps for High-Dimensional Imbalanced Data NEW

Imtiaz Ahmed, Hamdy Soliman — AI for Engineering (MDPI), Vol. 1, Issue 2, Article 10 — DOI: 10.3390/aieng1020010 (Published: 20 Aug, 2026)

Quantifies how partition stability, minority class recovery, and topological interpretability trade off across K-means, SOMs, consensus K-means, and four modern baselines on 10,095 TCGA cancer RNA samples (13,634 genes, 19 cancer types). At matched prototype budgets, K-means outperforms SOMs on both stability and minority recovery, while SOMs uniquely provide 2D topological visualization. No single method optimizes all three properties.
